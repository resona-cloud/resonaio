Resona — Methodology

How We Build.
Every Time.

Every engagement — regardless of industry, size, or product — runs through the same four phases. No shortcuts, no skipped steps. This is what happens at each one.

This is Resona's own operating cycle — how we run every engagement, from a first conversation to a system that runs without us. It's the same structure whether we're building a consumer app, standing up e-commerce for a legacy retailer, or shipping a platform like ARC or Procurely.

Phase 01

Discovery — Reading the Reality

We start with a structured discovery conversation, not a sales pitch. Where is the business actually today — technically, financially, competitively? This isn't a questionnaire, it's a working session where we pressure-test the assumptions you've been building on, so the plan that follows is built on what's actually true, not what's comfortable to believe. We map the honest starting point before proposing a single solution.

You leave with
A documented reality check — the gaps, the risks, and the actual starting line, in writing, before any solution gets proposed.
Phase 02

Architecture — Building the Foundation

Before anything launches, we design the system: operational frameworks, decision points, and the strategic foundation the business will stand on. Architecture is not a phase we rush through to get to the fun part — it is the foundation everything else depends on. We map how decisions get made and where the structural risk actually lives, before a single asset gets designed.

You leave with
A decision framework and operating structure — the blueprint everything downstream gets built against.
Phase 03

Brand & Execution — Built in Parallel

Brand identity and product execution happen simultaneously, not sequentially — name, visual language, product positioning, and a finished product, moving together so nothing arrives out of sync. A product without a brand is just software; a brand without a product is just a logo. We build both at once so what you ship and how you're perceived are never out of alignment.

You leave with
A named brand, a visual identity, and a shipped product — built to the same architecture, on the same timeline.
Phase 04

Systemize — Handing You the Keys

We install the methodology, documentation, and operating capacity into your organization so the system keeps running after we step back. This is the phase most studios skip or treat as a wrap-up — we treat it as the point of the engagement. You leave with a company, not a deliverable.

You leave with
Documentation, a runbook, and a team equipped to operate, maintain, and extend what we built — without us.

Working With Us

What It Actually Looks Like, Day to Day.

The four phases above are what happens to the work. This is what happens to you — from the first call to long after we've stepped back.

Step 01

Discovery Calls — Understanding the Business

Discovery isn't a single call — it's as many as it takes to actually understand the business. Every conversation covers the same ground: how stakeholders actually see the product today, what they expect from it, the current product strategy, and the execution details that will matter once we're building.

Step 02

Parallel Mapping — What We're Doing While You Talk

While those conversations are happening, we're already working on our side — mapping stakeholder milestones, the essential functions of the current product, and anything else we need before proposing a direction. Discovery and internal mapping run at the same time, not one after the other.

Step 03

Proposal & Fast Onboarding

Because discovery and mapping already happened in parallel, the proposal isn't a guess — it's built on what we actually found. Once it's signed, most engagements are on the road and actively building within 7 days. That speed comes from the work already done, not from skipping steps.

Step 04

Setting the Cadence

We ask about working preferences in the first several meetings — some stakeholders want a hands-off experience and check in at milestones; others want to be part of the process incrementally. Either way, we set the cadence around that from the start, so you're never guessing where things stand.

Step 05

The Build Loop — Iterate, Review, Refine, Repeat

This isn't a straight line from kickoff to delivery. Each milestone — architecture, design, strategy — runs through the same loop: we build a piece of it, put it in front of you as an MVP, an iteration, or a deck, get your input, and refine. We repeat that loop as many times as a milestone needs before moving to the next one. Nothing ships to you once and waits for a final verdict months later.

Step 06

Handoff — The 30-Day Touchbase

There's no single fixed engagement length — every scope is different. What doesn't change is what happens at the end: a 30-day touchbase with a real person after final delivery, to make sure the handoff actually holds up in practice, not just on paper.

Step 07

Long-Term Support

After the 30-day touchbase, ongoing support shifts to a dedicated support tool we're building — designed to keep your team on strategy, answer questions on demand, and give you a direct line back to us whenever something needs a human.
