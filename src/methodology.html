Resona — Methodology

How We Build.
Every Time.

Every engagement, whatever the industry, size, or product, goes through the same four phases. Here's what happens in each one.

This is how we run every engagement, from the first conversation to the handoff. It's the same structure whether we're building a consumer app, setting up e-commerce for a legacy retailer, or launching something like ARC or Procurely.

Phase 01

Discovery: Reading the Reality

We start with a conversation, not a sales pitch. Where does your business actually stand today: technically, financially, and against competitors? It's a working session, not a questionnaire. Together we test the assumptions you've been building on, so the plan that follows rests on what's true and not on what's comfortable to believe. We map that honest starting point before we suggest any solution.

You leave with
A written reality check: the gaps, the risks, and where you're really starting from, before any solution is proposed.
Phase 02

Architecture: Building the Foundation

Before anything launches, we work out how the product will run: the operating frameworks, the decision points, and the strategy it will stand on. It's tempting to rush this and get to the fun part. We don't, because everything after it depends on it. We map how decisions get made and where the real risk sits before a single asset gets designed.

You leave with
A decision framework and operating structure, the blueprint everything after it gets built against.
Phase 03

Brand & Execution: Built in Parallel

If your product needs a brand, we build it alongside the product: the name, the visual language, the positioning, and the finished product all moving together so nothing arrives out of step. A strong brand helps a product earn trust, so we shape how it looks and sounds while we're still building it. What you ship and how people see it should match.

You leave with
Where it's in scope: a named brand, a visual identity, and a shipped product, built to the same architecture on the same timeline.
Phase 04

Systemize: Handing You the Keys

We set up the methodology and documentation with your team so the product keeps running well after we step back. Plenty of studios skip this phase or treat it as a wrap-up. We treat it as a core part of the engagement, because a product your team can't run isn't really finished. By the end, you can run it without us.

You leave with
Documentation, a runbook, and a team that can operate, maintain, and extend what we built without us.

Working With Us

What It Actually Looks Like, Day to Day.

The four phases above are what happens to the work. Here's what happens for you, from the first call to long after we've stepped back.

Step 01

Discovery Calls: Understanding the Business

Discovery takes as many calls as it takes to really understand the business. Each one covers the same ground: how stakeholders see the product today, what they expect from it, the current product strategy, and the execution details that will matter once we're building.

Step 02

Parallel Mapping: What We're Doing While You Talk

While those conversations are happening, we're already working on our side: mapping stakeholder milestones, the essential functions of the current product, and anything else we need before proposing a direction. Discovery and our internal mapping run at the same time.

Step 03

Proposal & Fast Onboarding

Because discovery and mapping already happened in parallel, the proposal is built on what we actually found, not on a guess. Once it's signed, most engagements are up and building within 7 days. That speed comes from the work already done, and we haven't skipped any steps to get there.

Step 04

Setting the Cadence

In the first several meetings we ask how you like to work. Some people want a hands-off experience and check in at milestones. Others want to be part of the process as it goes. Either way, we set the pace around that from the start, so you're never guessing where things stand.

Step 05

The Build Loop: Iterate, Review, Refine, Repeat

The work doesn't run in a straight line from kickoff to delivery. Each milestone (architecture, design, strategy) goes through the same loop: we build a piece, show it to you as an MVP, an iteration, or a deck, get your input, and refine it. We repeat that as many times as the milestone needs before moving on. You're never handed one big thing months later and asked for a verdict.

Step 06

Handoff: The 30-Day Touchbase

Engagements don't have a fixed length because every scope is different. What stays the same is the ending: a 30-day touchbase with a real person after final delivery, to make sure the handoff holds up in practice and not just on paper.

Step 07

Long-Term Support

After the 30-day touchbase, ongoing support moves to a dedicated support tool. It keeps your team on strategy, answers questions on demand, and gives you a direct line back to us when something needs a human. It gets sharper with every engagement we run.
